App icon for "NPNnote", a note-taking app whose notes never leave the user's own browser. Motto: "If others can see it, it isn't a note."
Design: a flat monogram on a pure white square background. A bold, geometric capital "N" built from two thick vertical bars and one diagonal, in solid near-black (#111111). The diagonal is replaced by the edge of a page that folds over, so the N doubles as a closed notebook seen from the side. Exactly one letter, no other text, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D. Generous padding (mark occupies about 60% of the canvas). Must stay recognizable at 16x16. Confident and minimal, not a generic stock icon.
