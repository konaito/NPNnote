App icon for "NPNnote", a note-taking app whose notes never leave the user's own browser. Motto: "If others can see it, it isn't a note."
Design: a flat abstract mark on a pure white square background. A solid near-black (#111111) rounded rectangle like a closed notebook cover, with three short horizontal text lines visible only through a small round window near the top, as if you can only see in when you hold it yourself. No text or letters, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D. Generous padding (mark occupies about 60% of the canvas). Must stay recognizable at 16x16. Calm, minimal, slightly playful, not a generic stock icon.
