App icon for "NPNnote", a note-taking app whose notes never leave the user's own browser. Motto: "If others can see it, it isn't a note."
Design: a single flat geometric mark centered on a pure white square background. A note sheet with a folded top-right corner, drawn in solid near-black (#111111). The fold covers a small keyhole cut out of the sheet, so the note reads as private and closed. No text, no letters, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no outline stroke variations. Generous padding (mark occupies about 60% of the canvas). Must stay recognizable when scaled down to a 16x16 favicon. Modern, quiet, slightly unusual proportions rather than a generic stock icon.
